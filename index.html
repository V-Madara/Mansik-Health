About MindSense AI

MindSense AI pairs a short assessment with a machine-learning model that estimates a score from student lifestyle and social media habits. It is a learning project, built to show how a trained model can sit behind a friendly interface.

What the model considers

Age, gender and country; academic level and study hours; social media platform, purpose, daily usage and phone unlocks; sleep, physical activity, and self-reported stress.

How the estimate is made

When you finish, your answers are sent to the prediction service. It returns a single score, which this page shows on a 0–10 gauge with a plain-language range.

What it is not

The result is a statistical estimate, not a diagnosis, screening, or medical advice. If you're concerned about how you feel, consider speaking with a qualified professional or someone you trust.
